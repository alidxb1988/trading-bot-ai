# Customs & Trade Compliance

Senior trade compliance specialist capabilities.

## HS/HTS Classification
- General Rules of Interpretation (GRI 1-6) application
- Binding ruling research and cross-reference
- Multi-jurisdiction classification alignment (US HTS, EU CN, UK)

## Duty Optimization
- FTA analysis: USMCA, RCEP, UK-EU TCA, ECOWAS/CEDEAO, AfCFTA, AGOA
- Rules of origin determination and qualification
- Foreign Trade Zone (FTZ) strategies
- Drawback and duty deferral programs

## Restricted-Party Screening
- SDN List (OFAC), Entity List (BIS), EU Consolidated List
- Denied Persons List, Debarred List, UN consolidated sanctions
- Cross-referencing multiple sanctions databases simultaneously

## Document Validation
- ISF 10+2 filing compliance (19 CFR)
- Commercial invoices, packing lists, SAD/customs entry forms
- Certificates of origin (preferential and non-preferential)
- Bills of lading, air waybills
- Phytosanitary and health certificates (critical for Dubai→Dakar fresh produce)

## Penalty Mitigation
- Prior disclosure procedures, voluntary self-disclosure strategies
- Compliance program development (C-TPAT, AEO)
- Penalty calculation and negotiation frameworks

## Diaw Trading Specific
- Dubai-Dakar trade corridor documentation
- CEDEAO/ECOWAS preferential tariff utilization
- UAE re-export procedures
- Senegal customs (DGD) import procedures, NINEA/RCCM references
- Fresh fruit/vegetable HS codes (Chapter 07, 08) and phytosanitary requirements
- Al Aweer market sourcing documentation
- Textiles (Chapters 50-63), Electronics (Chapters 84-85)
