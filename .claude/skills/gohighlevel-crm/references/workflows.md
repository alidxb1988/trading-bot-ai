# GoHighLevel Pre-Built Workflows

1. **Monday Morning Pipeline Review**
   "Give me a pipeline summary for this week. How many deals at each stage, total value per stage, and which deals have been stuck for more than 5 days?"

2. **Lead Quality Audit**
   "Pull all leads from the last 30 days. How many booked a call? How many showed up? How many converted? Break it down by lead source."

3. **Stale Lead Reactivation**
   "Find all contacts tagged 'Discovery Call No-Show' from the last 60 days who are not currently in an active workflow. Tag them 'Reactivation' and give me a count."

4. **Weekly Client Report**
   "Create a report: new contacts added, calls booked, deals closed, pipeline value change, and top-performing traffic source. Format for email."

5. **Contact Cleanup**
   "Find duplicate contacts based on email address. List them with their tags and pipeline stage so I can decide which to merge."

6. **Funnel Analysis**
   "How many people hit the discovery call funnel landing page this month? How many booked? What is the page-to-booking conversion rate?"

7. **Revenue Reconciliation**
   "Pull all deals marked 'Closed Won' this month. Total revenue, average deal size, and close rate compared to last month."

8. **VIP Re-Engagement**
   "Search for contacts tagged 'VIP' who haven't been contacted in 30 days, then send them a personalized SMS about our new premium service offering."

9. **Inventory Alert**
   "List all products with low inventory, create a restock notification campaign, and send it to contacts tagged 'inventory-manager'."

10. **Social Media Campaign**
    "Create a social media post announcing our seasonal sale, schedule it for all connected platforms."

11. **Marketing Re-Engagement**
    "Find all contacts who opened our last email campaign but didn't purchase, add them to the 'warm-leads' workflow, and schedule a follow-up sequence."

12. **Diaw Trading — Supply Chain Pipeline**
    "Create a new opportunity in the 'Dubai to Dakar Supply Chain' pipeline for [product]. Add HS code, container size, and incoterm to custom fields. Schedule follow-up with customs broker."

13. **Gomale Group — B2B Acquisition**
    "Search for all contacts from the last 7 days tagged 'trade-show-lead'. Create opportunities in the B2B pipeline, assign to Aliou Diaw, and send introductory email sequence."
